verification) raised 60 findings against revision 1 of [architecture.md](architecture.md) on 17 October

exactly as the reviewer proposed; the reason for the difference is given. No finding was rejected.

## Effects on the plan that need the owner

Four changes touch the owner's decisions and are listed in architecture §12 with their safe defaults:
signature bytes not committed (D40); `import-legacy` keeps legacy entries (D51); a hand-moved border
across a short branch prints that branch raw (D15); landing phase 2 on a failed held-out gate needs the
owner's decision (D53).
